## Thread opener

A video is sampled cell by cell, translated into a living field of characters, and pushed through a slow wave of light.

A small note on the technique: One WebGL 2 fragment shader samples an muxed video texture on a monospaced character grid. Rec.601 luma selects from the ordered glyph ramp ` .:-+*=%@#`; the video's RGB feeds the ink.
